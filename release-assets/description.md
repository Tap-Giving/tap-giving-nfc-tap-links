Tap.Giving NFC Tap Disc Links is the free Chrome extension that changes where a Tap.Giving tap disc points, right from the page you are already on.

THE DISCS BEHIND IT

Tap.Giving tap discs are physical 4-inch discs with an NTAG213 chip inside, each one pre-encoded with its own go.tap.giving link. They live where people already are: on seat backs, walls, and welcome tables, in sanctuaries, lobbies, and classrooms. A guest taps a disc with a phone and lands on the page you chose: your giving page, a welcome page, an event page.

Pages change. A new giving page, a seasonal event, a moved welcome page. When that page changes, the disc should change with it, and that is the job this extension does.

FREE COMPANION TO THE REDIRECT DASHBOARD

Every disc's destination lives in the Tap.Giving Redirect Dashboard at go.tap.giving, which is free. The dashboard is where you manage all your tap links. This extension is the shortcut for the moment you are standing on the new page and want a disc to point at it now.

WHO IT IS FOR

Churches and ministries that use Tap.Giving tap discs for tap-to-give donations and guest connections, and the pastors, administrators, and communications staff who keep the church's pages current.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Open the page the disc should open.
2. Click the extension and pick the tap link.
3. Confirm. The extension writes the new destination, reads the link back, and shows you the verified result.

WHY INSTALL IT

- Change a destination from the page itself. You are already looking at the page you want a disc to open; set it right there, without opening the dashboard, finding the link, and pasting an address.
- No setup and nothing new to sign into. It uses the Redirect Dashboard sign-in you already have in your browser. If you are signed out, it offers one button to sign in.
- Verified on the spot. After you confirm, the extension reads the link back and shows you the destination it now points to.
- Built to touch nothing else. It writes exactly one thing, the destination of a link you pick, in your own dashboard, after you confirm it.

WHAT IT ACCESSES, AND WHY

- The address of the tab you click it on, only at that moment, because that address is the destination you are setting.
- Your dashboard session for go.tap.giving only, so the dashboard knows whose links to show. It never sees your password, contacts no other site, runs no analytics, and stores nothing.

Free, always. It never sees your password, and it works with the free Tap.Giving Redirect Dashboard you already use.
